Direction diagnosis and correction of the prior review:
The v1.3 review mislabeled the vendor native masks as cardinal directions.
ReadJoystick alone proves GPIO 200/201/202/203 -> 10/40/80/20 hex, but not
the direction names. The actual stock joystick_input callback and default
joy_key_mask table establish libretro IDs 4/5/6/7 -> 10/40/80/20, respectively.
Therefore physical GPIO order is UP/DOWN/LEFT/RIGHT. Its analog branch also
uses 10/40 for negative/positive vertical and 80/20 for horizontal.
See joystick_input.asm and joy_key_mask.hex, plus the earlier ReadJoystick.asm.
