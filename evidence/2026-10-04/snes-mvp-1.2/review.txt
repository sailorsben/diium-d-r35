Confirmed source defect:
Stock ReadJoystick at 0x1599c reads GPIO 0x200 UP, 0x201 LEFT, 0x202 RIGHT,
0x203 DOWN (native masks 0x10,0x40,0x80,0x20). MVP had the final three assigned
DOWN,LEFT,RIGHT respectively. Corrected against the actual stock executable;
see ReadJoystick.asm. This defect alone does not explain every reported symptom.

Verified stock compatibility contract:
main/vrtemu ShareMemCreat uses shared-memory key 1234, size 460, IPC_CREAT|0666.
vrtemu xintiao increments word 1 and sets word 0 to a 60-tick liveness budget.
The MVP previously omitted that publication. v1.3 preserves it on the input
polling path, throttled to 15 ms, without a separate worker or watchdog disable.
Only words 0 and 1 are written; detach does not remove the shared segment.
This contract is proven for stock main/vrtemu. /wdt's own implementation and
whether any watchdog caused this fallback remain unconfirmed.

v1.3 changes and verification:
- Corrected D-pad mapping; actual board decoder tested under ARM/QEMU for
  vendor D-pad/face/shoulder pins, MENU chord and GPIO read failure behavior.
- Heartbeat layout/counter/rate and preservation of other fields/owners tested
  in an isolated IPC_PRIVATE segment, never the production key on the test host.
- Persistent bounded library/pause input, exit request and display cleanup logs.
  No per-frame gameplay input logging.
- Wrapper copies /wdt and /power_key read-only and records boot/IPC state.
  Three runtime snapshots capture thread/process state and RAM output, then
  monitoring ends. Monitor cancellation and stale-snapshot clearing are checked.
- Existing 180-frame exact Plus core ZIP run, audio transport/accounting,
  saves/snapshots/rollback, UI, scan and startup/splash checks all passed.
Physical input/audio/display endurance remains pending; no speed claim.
