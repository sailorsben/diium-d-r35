Source connection:
After the first frame and input baseline, the library polls then calls
board_sleep_until(board_now_ns()+8,000,000). The old board clock used libc
clock_gettime; the old sleep sent that timestamp directly to kernel
clock_nanosleep(CLOCK_MONOTONIC,TIMER_ABSTIME). The captured flags are absolute.
The intended 8 ms wait is the only main-thread timer wait in this library path.
The long unchanged sleep and clock discrepancy identify the timing seam as
the immediate failure mechanism. Exact libc/vDSO versus direct syscall clock
readings were not captured in v1.3; v1.4 records them at startup to verify the
underlying clock discrepancy. Do not claim a particular vDSO/kernel bug proven.
