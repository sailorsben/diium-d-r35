D-R35 LAB 2 / INFRASTRUCTURE CONTRACTS

One automatic run, about 60-90 seconds; supervised deadline 120 seconds.
No game loads. Your game runner, ROMs, saves and stock software are preserved.

1. Safely eject D:, put the card back in the handheld and boot normally.
2. Let LAB 2 finish. It tests idle/loaded timers, memory, five audio transports,
   then timer/device pacing with steady and occasional longer CPU work.
3. Tones should fade at their ends. Labels are now drawn at their real resolution.
   The moving pacing screens identify the rate and steady/burst work.
4. Normal completion saves logs and returns to the regular launcher.
5. Power off normally, reconnect D: and tell us about sound, labels and handoff.

MENU can cancel between timer groups and audio profiles, or between paced calls.
If the supervisor has to force termination, it waits for the child to exit and
holds for reboot. The next reboot is stock because both one-shot markers were
consumed. A kernel-blocked child requires reboot; no competing display starts.

This tests infrastructure behavior, not FF6's full-speed acceptance.
