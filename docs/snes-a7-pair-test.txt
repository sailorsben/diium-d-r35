SNES 1.19-pair1 -- separate A7 renderer comparison (measurement only)

1. Safely eject I: (DIIUM D-R35), insert it into the handheld, and cold-boot.
2. Let it run automatically. Do not navigate menus or manually select Bio Blast.
   It replays Bio Blast and two later sections: baseline, combined, combined,
   baseline in each section, 12 independent passes total. Some passes may stop
   audio early; the next pass is a separate measurement, not playback recovery.
3. Wait for it to finish and return to the stock launcher. MENU aborts the suite.
4. Record whether the complete-effect passes looked correct, whether sound was
   clean, whether the device powered off, and whether it returned to the launcher.
5. Power down normally, reconnect the card, and report its current drive letter.
   Do not reboot for another test: this one-shot is consumed on the first boot.
