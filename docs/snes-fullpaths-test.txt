SNES 1.19-fullpaths1 — measurement only, every frame

Safely eject I:, insert the card and cold boot the D-R35.
No B press or menu selection is required. The one-shot starts automatically.
Let it run until the stock launcher returns. Allow about 40 minutes; the full
100-run schedule is 33.4 minutes of gameplay plus loading/flushing. Failed runs
can shorten the test. Keep the device powered throughout.

Repeated effects are intentional. The suite tests baseline, fixed-color rows,
subscreen composition, DSP dead-work removal, and all three combined. Each
plays Holy, Meteor, Meltdown, Siren, Bahamut, Carbunkl, Quasar, Lullaby Rage,
Bio Blast and Confuser. The second pass reverses case and variant order.

Every SNES frame and audio sample is retained. Fatal errors remain visible;
after orderly worker cleanup, the next independent run starts. Per-frame data
stays in RAM; files are flushed between runs after the workers stop. Headroom,
valid pointer accounting, frequency availability and frame completion are logged.
This is an experiment, not a production replacement or a universal guarantee.

MENU or Select+Start requests an early stop. Wait for the stock launcher before
powering off, then reconnect I:. The next cold boot takes the stock path because
the one-shot marker was consumed. Original ROM, saves and production cores remain
protected; all new progress and diagnostics are in retro/snes-fullpaths1.
