SNES 1.19-profile1: quick A7 CPU breakdown (measurement only)

Safely eject I:, insert it and cold-boot. No B press is needed.
Allow about 2 minutes; wait for the stock launcher to return, then power off
before reconnecting the card. MENU stops safely.

Four automatic epochs, at most 800 calls each:
1 Meteor control; 2 Meteor with sparse phase clocks;
3 Quasar with sparse phase clocks; 4 Quasar control.
The known audio failure can end an epoch early. That is recorded and the
next epoch starts; a successful animation is not the benchmark verdict.

Same latest combined core in every epoch, all video frames and audio retained.
This is a profiler, not a new rendering fix. Original ROM and saves preserved.
The one-shot marker is consumed before launch; later boots are stock.
