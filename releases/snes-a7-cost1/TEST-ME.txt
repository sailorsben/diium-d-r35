SNES cost1: measurement only

This boot automatically runs13 independent replay passes: three controls,
three sparse PPU profiles, six warm hot-path benchmarks, and one dense
frame320 cross-check. Every pass starts from the same separate Bio Blast
snapshot and replays the same input. No menu selection or game input is needed.

The attack may fail and restart between passes. Those are separate experiments,
not successful audio recovery. Wait for the stock launcher. Allow about three
minutes, including log flushing. Select+Start aborts the current suite; wait
for the stock launcher before the physical power button.

After powering off, reconnect D:. Archive with build/collect-a7-cost.py before
any writes. The consumed marker means the next reboot is stock. No production
window candidate is installed and neither SNES adapter/core is replaced.

Do not power off while a replay or flush is running. Measurement results are
pending until collection; this suite is not an audio fix.
